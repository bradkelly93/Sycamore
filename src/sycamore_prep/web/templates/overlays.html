Market overlays — extra context

These are optional, market-opinion lenses: what the options market thinks about risk, what the price chart is doing, and what prediction markets imply about specific events. They're useful color — but, on purpose, none of them ever change a stock's score or rank. The investment case stays bottom-up.

CONTEXT ONLY  Prediction markets (Polymarket) — implied odds of company / industry / macro events. A way to sanity-check event risk, never a score input. Tick a box to confirm a market belongs to a name — it saves to the mapping file instantly, and your edits are never overwritten by re-discovery. {% include "_mapping_table.html" %}
CONTEXT ONLY  Volatility & technical trend attach to individual names. To see them, run the Screener with the “Volatility” / “Technical trend” boxes ticked, or open a Company deep-dive — the options-market risk read appears there, clearly walled off from the valuation.
